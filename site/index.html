          Your Timeline

          1. Android: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data.
          2. iPhone: Google Maps → your profile picture → Settings → Personal content → Export Timeline data.
          3. Tap Import below and pick the file. Stops from the last 90 days land in your swipe deck.

          The file is read on this phone and nothing is uploaded. Google’s export has place IDs and coordinates but no names, so name each restaurant once (“Open in Maps” shows what it is) and tap “Not food” on anything else. Home and work are skipped automatically. Re-import anytime; visits you already have are not duplicated.
