            Account

            Your Timeline

            1. Android: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline → Export Timeline data.
            2. iPhone: Google Maps → your profile picture → Settings → Personal content → Export Timeline data.
            3. Tap Import below and pick the file. Stops from the last 90 days land in your swipe deck.

            The file is read on this phone and nothing is uploaded. Restaurants are named from an open map of places (Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap). The app downloads that map only for the areas you’ve been, about 25 km at a time. Each stop’s Google place ID is named by Google once (shared with other testers as a link to our open map, never with your name). Otherwise a stop counts only if you stayed 10+ minutes and a restaurant is within 8 m of it; everything else goes to Skipped places, where you can restore it. Home and work are skipped automatically. Re-import anytime; visits you already have are not duplicated.

            last import or re-check

              Re-check uses what this phone and the shared links already know, so it rarely needs Google. Restaurants marked “Google (not on our map yet)” can be kept with Name it.

              Automatic visit detection

                When you stay somewhere 10+ minutes, your phone checks it against the restaurant map. Restaurant visits become cards; every other stop is forgotten right away. When 5 visits are waiting, you get a reminder (at most once a day). No location history is kept on the phone or sent anywhere.

                Skipped places

                Stops the app decided weren’t meals. Tap Restore if one was a restaurant; it won’t be skipped again.
